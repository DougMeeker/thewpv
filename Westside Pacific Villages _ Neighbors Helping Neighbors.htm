Welcome to Westside Pacific Villages! 

WHO WE ARE: We are a vibrant and diverse member-driven community that empowers older adults to stay socially connected, active and independent so they can age successfully in their own homes.  

WHAT WE DO: We connect neighbors with neighbors to share social activities, skills and support with one another, to enrich our lives in every decade. We coordinate the efforts of our volunteers to provide many benefits including social activities, educational events, companionship, help around the house, transportation and more. 

If you’re looking to meet neighbors for friendship, shared interests, new experiences and to build a network of support as we take charge of how we age in community together, we’d love to meet you!   

WPV serves Westchester, Playa del Rey, Playa Vista, Marina del Rey, Del Rey, Mar Vista, Culver City, Ladera Heights, El Segundo, Manhattan Beach and adjacent areas.  

WE HAVE AN ENGAGING CALENDAR OF ACTIVITIES EACH MONTH. CLICK ON ‘WHAT WE DO” AND “EVENTS” TO SEE WHAT’S COMING UP!
