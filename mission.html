MISSION & VISION

Mission: Westside Pacific Villages (WPV) is a grassroots, membership-based, and volunteer-driven non-profit that empowers older adults to stay socially connected, active and independent as they age in place in their homes and neighborhoods. We connect neighbors with neighbors to support one another with a network of services and social activities.

Vision: To create a world where all people flourish and grow as they age, staying connected with care and support within their community.

The Movement: WPV is part of a nationwide movement of nearly 300 local Villages dedicated to redefining the way we age in place, and to creating more opportunities for people to thrive in their midlife and beyond journey. Villages are not physical locations or caregiving agencies. We are organized networks of local volunteers, members, staff and community partners who enjoy connecting with each other through activities, programs and support.
